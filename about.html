Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Yesel was founded in 2026 with a simple but powerful vision: to make quality STEM education accessible to students across Kenya. We believe that every young Kenyan deserves the opportunity to learn the skills that will define the future—electronics, coding, and robotics.

As a newly established organization, we bring fresh perspectives and modern teaching methodologies to STEM education. We understand the unique challenges and opportunities within the Kenyan educational landscape, and we've designed our programs to bridge the gap between traditional education and the demands of the 21st-century economy.

Our name "Yesel" represents our commitment to empowering the next generation. We're not just teaching technical skills—we're nurturing problem-solvers, innovators, and future leaders who will drive Kenya's technological advancement.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality STEM education that equips Kenyan students with practical skills in electronics, coding, and robotics, preparing them to thrive in the global digital economy while solving local challenges.

Our Vision

To be Kenya's leading STEM education provider, creating a generation of tech-savvy innovators who will position Kenya as a hub for technological innovation in Africa and beyond.

The Opportunity

Why Kenya Needs STEM Education

Kenya is rapidly emerging as a technology hub in Africa. With initiatives like Konza Technopolis and a thriving startup ecosystem, the demand for tech-skilled professionals continues to grow.

However, there's a significant gap between traditional education and the skills needed in the modern workforce. Yesel exists to bridge this gap by providing practical, hands-on STEM education that prepares students for real-world opportunities.

How We Teach

Our Teaching Approach

01
Learn by Doing

Every lesson includes hands-on projects. Students don't just learn theory—they build real circuits, write real code, and create real robots.

02
Progressive Learning

Our curriculum builds progressively, starting with fundamentals and advancing to complex projects as students develop their skills.

03
Small Class Sizes

We maintain small class sizes to ensure every student gets personalized attention and support throughout their learning journey.

04
Real-World Projects

Students work on projects that solve real problems, preparing them for actual challenges they'll face in their careers.

05
Collaborative Learning

Students learn to work in teams, developing communication and collaboration skills essential for the modern workplace.

06
Industry-Aligned

Our curriculum is designed with input from industry professionals to ensure students learn relevant, in-demand skills.
