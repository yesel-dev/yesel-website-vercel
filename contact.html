Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Enrolling is easy! Simply fill out the contact form above or reach out via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a brief consultation to understand your child's interests and recommend the best program. Once you've chosen a program, we'll guide you through the registration process.

No prior experience is required for our beginner programs! We start from the basics and build up progressively. For advanced courses, we'll assess the student's current level and recommend the appropriate starting point.

For most programs, we provide all necessary materials and equipment during class sessions. For coding classes, students benefit from having access to a computer at home for practice. We'll provide a detailed list of any requirements when you enroll.

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention. Typically, our classes have 8-12 students, with a maximum of 15 for lecture-style sessions. This allows our instructors to provide individual support when needed.

We conduct classes at various locations depending on the program. Contact us for information about class venues near you. For school partnerships, we bring the program directly to your school.

Yes! Some of our coding programs can be conducted online. However, for electronics and robotics, we strongly recommend in-person classes for the hands-on experience. Contact us to discuss available options.
